01

What the name must carry

Taken from the vault: Topic 02 Company definition, Topic 13 Future branches and the founders' meeting on 24 Sep.

The company

  • An agency that handles an organisation's full digital workload. Event design is the first service line, not the whole company.
  • Every engagement can end with a guideline for what comes next.
  • Wants to be known for reliability, quality and understanding the Gulf market.
  • Later branches: a creator brand, consumer products, software, new regions, training.

So the name should

  • Work in Arabic and English, and be easy to say in both.
  • Not lock the company into events, marketing, media or AI.
  • Stretch into a family: Studio, Digital, Labs, Ventures.
  • Be clear of existing Saudi trade names and trademarks (Ministry of Commerce and SAIP).

Greek words

Source: ChatGPT · Greek dictionary search

Ancient Greek words for the journey from thought to form to execution. The literal Modern Greek words for "creativity" and "inspiration" (Dimiourgikotita, Empnefsi) are accurate but too awkward to use as company names.

  • PLASIS: Shape what comes next.

Literal Modern Greek

Favourite from this round: EPINOIA, with PLASIS as the more unusual alternative.

05

Names from the methodology

Source: ChatGPT · built from the vault's phase-and-gate method

A gate-based method: turn a need into a concept, make it through real client work, measure the result, learn from the evidence, and expand only when demand, money and capability support the next step.

01 · ἔννοια
Ennoia
Define and understand the concept
02 · ποίησις
Poiesis
Create it and bring it into existence
03 · πρᾶξις
Praxis
Carry it out on a real client project
04 · μέτρον
Metron
Measure hours, cost, failures and client satisfaction
05 · σύνεσις
Synesis
Combine the evidence into understanding
06 · τέλος
Telos
Decide: continue, change or stop

Five principles

  • Real work before assumptions
  • Evidence before expansion
  • Results before time: a phase ends when the outcome is reached
  • Growth opens only when capability, money or clients allow it
  • Structured change: service, customer, price or package

KAIROS, the right moment: the next phase opens only when the conditions are right.

Method names

  • The Ennoia–Praxis Method: From concept to execution, guided by evidence.
  • The Metron Method: Create. Test. Measure. Evolve.

Strongest words from the method: Metron, Praxis, Synesis, Epinoia, Kairos.
